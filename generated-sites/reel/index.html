A festival of short animation in one hall, with no parallel programme: everyone in the city watches the same film at the same minute, and argues about it afterwards in the same corridor.

Buy a three-night pass See the programme

Four programmes, and nothing runs twice.

Films are grouped by what they do to a room, not by country or technique. Each block is under ninety minutes, because that is how long an audience can watch drawings without going quiet in the wrong way.

How selection worked, in detail

Who chose, and what they argued about

Five people watched all 641 submissions between January and April. Every film that got two yes votes went to the long list; every rejection is written down and available to the director who asks.

Марина ОдинецAnimator, twenty-two years of cut-out. Argued for keeping the two films that made people leave.
Дмитрий ШеинSound designer. Rejected four films for their soundtracks alone and wrote a page about each.
Aino KärkiProgrammer from Tampere. Insisted that the children's block be selected by the same standard as the rest.
Тимур ГареевRuns a two-person studio. The only one who watched everything twice, on a laptop, on the train.
Лена БахTeaches at the school that sent nine submissions; recused herself from all nine.

The hall, and how to find it

House of Culture, Zavodskaya 4Built 1957, 312 seats, a balcony that squeaks and which we have decided to leave alone.
ProjectionDCP and 16 mm. Three films this year are on film and will be threaded in front of you.
Getting thereTram 4 to Zavodskaya, then two minutes. Parking behind the building is free after 18:00.
AccessStep-free entrance from the yard side, four wheelchair spaces, subtitles on every non-silent film.
The corridorTea, no alcohol, and one long table where the directors sit after their block whether or not anybody comes.
If it sells outTwenty standing places go on sale an hour before each screening, for 300 ₽, cash at the door.
