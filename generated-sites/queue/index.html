We are not a hype account

A drop is an announcement with a countdown attached. Everything else — how many pieces exist, who got them, what they cost a week later — is left in the gray area, and the gray area is where the money is made.

QUEUE publishes the boring half. Piece counts when a label states them, and the word unstated when it does not. Queue times in your timezone. Resale spread eleven days on, whether that flatters the label or not.

We take no cut of any sale, run no affiliate links, and accept no paid placement in the week table. When a label refuses to state a number, that refusal is the entry.

Method

01

We watch, not guess

Every entry starts from a label's own channel — the store page, the newsletter, the notice board. No leaks, no reposts, no screenshots of screenshots.

02

Counts or silence

If the piece count is published, it goes in the table. If it is not, the cell reads unstated, and that stays visible forever in the archive.

03

Eleven days later

Every drop gets revisited on day eleven, when the resale market has stopped being excited and started being a market.
