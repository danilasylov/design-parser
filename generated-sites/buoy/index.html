The course

Saturday is charts and rules. Sunday is the wheel.

Nothing is taught twice, and nothing is taught only in a classroom. Every rule you learn on Saturday you break — deliberately, under supervision — on Sunday, so you find out what it actually protects.

01

The boat as a system

Hull, engine, fuel, bilge, battery. What you check before you cast off and why the checklist has fourteen lines.
Classroom, then dockside on a boat with the floorboards up
4 h + 4 h
02

Charts, buoys, and the depth you cannot see

Lateral and cardinal marks, sectored lights, reading a paper chart against the plotter and noticing when they disagree.
Classroom, then a daylight passage across the reservoir
4 h + 4 h

Right of way, in traffic

Crossing, overtaking, narrow channels, and the barge that legally has all of them. Sound signals you will actually hear.
The commercial channel on a Sunday afternoon, on purpose

Open water with the rescue service standing by

Pre-departure

The fourteen lines you will know by heart

Printed on the boat, laminated, and checked out loud by whoever is at the helm. By the fourth weekend nobody reads it — and we still make them say it.

Four boats

Two 5.5-metre open boats, one cabin cruiser, one with a tiller so you learn what a wheel hides from you.

Two per boat

You steer half the time and watch the other half, which is where most of the learning happens.

The exam

What the examiner actually asks

The theory bank is public and has 480 questions. The practical is twenty minutes and has four elements. We publish both, because a course that keeps them mysterious is selling anxiety.

Price

One price, and the state fee is inside it

No separate charge for fuel, for the boat, for the exam booking, or for the retake. The only thing you buy separately is a lifejacket that fits you, and we will tell you which one.

  • Do I need to be able to swim?Yes, and we ask you to demonstrate it in the first weekend — fifty metres in a lifejacket, calmly.
  • What if I miss a weekend?You join the same weekend in the next group at no cost. Most people miss one; the schedule assumes it.
  • Is there a boat afterwards?Graduates rent ours at half price for the first season, on the condition that the checklist is filed after every trip.
  • Winter groups?Theory runs from January, water from late April. The lake decides, not us.
