Yes. Change the period, switch the workspace view, hover data points, move the forecast slider, or download the sample CSV. All calculations run in your browser.

Can I pause the animations?

Use Pause motion in the footer. Your system’s reduced-motion preference is also respected, including changes made while the page is open.
