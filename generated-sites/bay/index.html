House rules, all of them

Noise until eightMachines run 07:00 to 20:00 on weekdays, 10:00 to 18:00 on Saturdays, never on Sundays. The neighbours were here first.
Sweep your own floorThere is no cleaner for the workshops, only for the stairs and the yard. Dust that leaves your floor is your dust in someone else's glaze.
One trade per floorSubletting to a different trade needs everybody's yes, because the extraction, the insurance and the smell are shared.
Show what you makeTwo open days a year, the first Saturday in May and the first in November. Doors open, machines on, everyone in the building takes part.
