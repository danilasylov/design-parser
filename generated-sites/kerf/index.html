Tolerances

The numbers we will actually hold

Cut accuracy±0.1 mm on the contour up to 6 mm plate; ±0.2 mm above it. Measured on the first article of every batch, not on a sample from last year.
Bend angle±0.5° with a measured first bend. Anything tighter is a conversation, and usually the answer is a jig rather than a promise.
EdgeNitrogen cut on stainless and aluminium, so the edge takes paint without grinding. Oxygen on thick mild steel, and we say so on the quote.
MaterialsS235, S355, AISI 304 and 430, AlMg3, brass on request. We hold the first four on the rack; the rest adds four days.
Minimum orderThere is none. One part is a normal order and gets the same first-article measurement as four hundred.
What we refuseGalvanised sheet, anything painted, and drawings without a material specified. The first two poison the machine, the third poisons the schedule.
