Two people, one bicycle, and absolutely no talking.

Get Tandem €14 · one purchase, two players, no accounts

One of you pedals. One of you steers. Neither of you can do both.

Tandem is a co-op game for two people sitting at one keyboard, and it takes away the one thing co-op games always give you: the ability to explain yourself.

The pedaller

Left hand, two keys, alternating. Rush and the chain slips. Slow down on a hill and you roll backwards into whatever you just passed.

The steerer

Right hand, three keys, and the only view of the road. You can see the pothole. You cannot say the word pothole.

The rule

The microphone stays off and the game knows it. Speak and the bell rings — a small, disappointed sound your friend will hear too.

Twelve rides, one summer

Each ride is fifteen minutes long and ends where the next one starts. Nothing unlocks, nothing is missable, and there is no shop.
