How it works

Three taps, no queue

Encore only lists rooms under four hundred capacity. If a gig needs a stadium and a presale, it is somebody else's app.

1Open it after workEverything within a half-hour walk, sorted by door time rather than by hype.
2Tap once to holdA place is held for twenty minutes. No account until you actually decide to go.
3Show the door your phoneThe ticket is a code, works offline, and refunds itself if the gig is cancelled.
