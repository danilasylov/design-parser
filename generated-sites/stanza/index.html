STANZA

Two-week laboratory in interaction and media design

12—23 May 2026 · Sixteen places

12—23 MAY

Stanza is a two-week laboratory in interaction and media design. Sixteen participants, five tutors, one question: what does a screen owe the person standing in front of it?

No lectures. Mornings are studio work, afternoons are critique, evenings are whatever the group decides. Nothing is graded — everything is shown.

Field study 04 — moiré
