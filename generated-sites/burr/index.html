Every batch is written down

Twelve kilos at a time on a 1978 drum roaster we rebuilt ourselves. The curve, the drop temperature and the person who stood at the machine go on the bag — and on the website, whether the batch went well or not.

01

Green in

Bought a sack at a time from two importers we have met in person. Nothing sits longer than four months.

02

Tuesday drum

Roasted the morning it ships. First crack, drop time and weight loss are logged by hand in the same notebook since 2021.

03

Cupped Thursday

Every batch is tasted blind against the previous one. If it lost, it goes to the café downstairs and never to you.
