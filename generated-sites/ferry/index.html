Three calls, and the routing is somebody else's problem

Quote

Ask for a route and get every option back with the real cost, the real time and the bridge that will actually carry it.

Sign

One signature from the user's existing wallet. No new approvals, no intermediate token, no second confirmation halfway across.

Settle

We watch both sides and refund automatically if a leg fails. Median settlement is under two minutes across the eleven.

Eleven networks

The same call, whichever way the money goes

Chains are a parameter, not a rewrite. Adding a network on our side does not change a line on yours — the quote object keeps the shape it had on day one.
