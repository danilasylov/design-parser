We do not make it look new

A repair that hides is a repair that fails twice: once when the thread gives, and once when you find out the garment was never worth keeping. We work the other way. The darn sits on top in a colour you chose, the patch is square and the stitches are 4 mm apart because that is the distance at which a hand-made row reads as a decision.

Everything comes back with a card: what was done, what thread, how long it should hold, and what to bring it back for. Nine hundred and forty garments since we opened the room, and four returned for the same hole.

We take knitwear, denim, workwear, coats, canvas bags, one tent and, twice, a flag. We do not take shoes, leather or anything that has been in a fire — there are two people down the street who do those properly and we will give you the address.

Wool is charged by the hole, denim by the area, everything else by the half hour. If the repair costs more than the garment is worth we will say so before we start, and then usually do it anyway if you still want it.

Bring the hole

Drop-off is Wednesday to Saturday, 11:00 to 18:00, at the green door on Hamburger Straße 41. Leave the garment, take a numbered tag, get a message when it is done. Post works too — put the tag number on the outside and we will send it back the way it came.

Six chairs, every Tuesday

The chairs go up on the first of the month and are gone by the third. If you have never held a needle, take the chair marked first time — someone sits with you for the whole two hours and you leave with one finished hole.

Bring your own garment. We supply wool, cotton, needles, mushrooms, scissors and tea. Nobody photographs anybody. Nothing is posted anywhere.

Since 2021: three hundred and forty evenings, two thousand one hundred chairs, one hundred and eleven people who now mend at home and never come back, which is the point.
