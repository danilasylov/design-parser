Practice

Built for people who read carefully

Almanac does not summarise your work away. It keeps the sentence a person actually said and makes it easy to find again two years later.

01

Search by meaning

Ask “where did people give up?” and get the passages, not a keyword count. Every hit opens at the right second of audio.

02

Tags that stay honest

Suggested tags arrive as drafts. Nothing is applied to a transcript until a researcher accepts it — the record stays yours.

03

A page per study

Method, participants, findings and raw quotes live on one addressable page that stays readable long after the team has moved on.
