The room…

A single room above a bicycle shop with twenty-eight chairs, one pair of horns and no screen of any kind. It seats fewer people than the licence allows because the back row is where the sound stops being good.

No talking on a side

Between sides, in the corridor and at the bar, talk all you like. While the needle is down the only permitted sounds are the record and the room.

No phones out

Not a rule about photography, a rule about light. A screen at the back of a dark room reaches everyone in front of it.

Come for the whole thing

Doors close when side A starts and open again at the break. If you are late, the bar has your seat number and a stool.
