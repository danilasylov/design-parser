Open kilns
Monday 14

Wedging

Nine studios open their doors at eight in the morning and let anybody in until the clay runs out. No booking, no fee, aprons provided.

Tuesday 15

Throwing, badly

Twenty wheels in the market square, twenty potters standing behind them, and a queue of people who have never centred anything.

Wednesday 16

Glaze night

Eleven glaze recipes published, mixed in front of you, brushed onto test tiles you take home. The one that never works is demonstrated too.

€6, 18:00 →
Thursday 17

The long firing

The wood kiln is lit at four in the morning and fed in two-hour shifts for sixty hours. Sign up for a shift; bring gloves and something to read.

Shifts open →
Friday 18

Repair table

Bring the broken thing. Gold is not free, staples are, and the answer is sometimes that it should stay broken.

14:00—20:00 →
Sat 19 — Sun 20

Opening and market

The kiln is unpacked in front of everybody on Saturday at noon; whatever survived is on the tables by two, priced by whoever made it.

Tickets
Four of the seven days cost nothing at all.

The glaze night and the two workshop afternoons are €6 each, which is what the clay and the electricity cost us. A week pass is €14 and includes a cup you fired yourself, if it survives.
