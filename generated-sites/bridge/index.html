The fit is a measurement,
not a size.

A frame has six numbers. Four of them are yours and come out of a thirty-second scan on a phone: pupillary distance, bridge width, the angle your ears sit at, and how far the temple has to run before it turns down.

We print on Thursdays, in batches of forty. If your scan lands on Friday it waits — a batch that runs half empty costs more powder per frame, and that would end up in your price.

A scan that fails is refunded the same day. About one in nine fails, usually because of a fringe.
