We are an architecture practice that measures what is already standing.

Kombinat Hall, Rustavi — long section, existing frame retained 1:200 · surveyed 2024

Nothing we work on was designed to be temporary.

Plinth was founded in 2016 to do the unglamorous half of architecture: surveying post-war buildings that nobody has drawn since they were built, and finding out what they can still carry.

We take on a project only when the existing structure stays. Sometimes that means a new floor plate inside an old frame; sometimes it means the honest answer that the building needs nothing but a roof and a decade of patience.

Surveys

Before anything is drawn we measure. Hand survey first, laser scan second, and a written note of every place the two disagree — which on a building of this age is most of them.

The survey is published with the project. If we conclude that a building should be left alone, that conclusion is published too, with the drawings that led to it.
