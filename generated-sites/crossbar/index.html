Two days. Nine sports.

Crossbar is a weekend of street sport for people who never made a team. No rankings, no qualifiers, no coaches shouting from the line — you put your name down on Friday and you are on a court by Saturday morning.

Every court is public and stays public after the festival. What we bring is the schedule, the referees, the water and someone who knows the rules.

The nine

Street football

Five a side, rolling substitutions, no offside and no goalkeeper taller than the crossbar.

Both days

Ten kilometres

One flat loop along the river. Chip timing, but the results page only shows your own time.

Saturday

Basketball 3×3

Ten minutes or twenty-one points. Losers keep the court if nobody is waiting.

Both days

Bouldering

Twenty problems on an old wall, graded by whoever set them that morning.

Saturday

Table tennis

Six tables under floodlights, single elimination, bats provided and mostly warped.

Saturday night

Open water

Eight hundred metres in the reservoir, wetsuits allowed, medals aggressively not awarded.
