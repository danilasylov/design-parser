Questions we are asked at the door

Can I see the menu first?No. It is written the morning of the dinner and printed at four. Tell us what you cannot eat and we will cook around it.
What if I am late?We hold your chair until 20:15 and then the first plate goes out without you. You will catch up by the third course.
Do you take large groups?Up to six. Beyond that you are the whole room, which we also do — write to us and we will send the numbers.
Is there a wine list?There are four bottles open at any moment and a person who will tell you honestly which one goes with the fish.
