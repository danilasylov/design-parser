Four steps, and none of them is a phone call

A parametric policy is a bet with published rules. You choose the trigger before the season, the weather station reports, and the payout is arithmetic.

01

Pick the station

The nearest official gauge to your parcel, named in the policy with its number and its distance in kilometres.

02

Set the trigger

Rainfall under 42 mm across the flowering window, or four days above 32 °C in a row. You choose the number and see the price move.

03

Grow the season

Nothing to report, nothing to photograph. We watch the same public data you can open on your phone.

04

Get paid

The window closes, the number is read, the transfer goes out. Median five days, longest ever eleven.
