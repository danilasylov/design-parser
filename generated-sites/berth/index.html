What a berth actually is

A bed, not a seat

Sheets, a pillow and a blanket are made up before you board. The berth is 190 cm long, which is longer than most hotel beds are wide.

A door that locks

Every compartment locks from the inside, and the attendant holds your ticket overnight so nobody wakes you at the border.

Morning included

Coffee and bread arrive forty minutes before arrival, which is roughly when the light starts coming through the blind.
