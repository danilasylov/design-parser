ⓘAbout

    

      
We are a lab of nine people in Bodø, funded by a shipping consortium and the Norwegian Environment Agency. We keep fourteen hydrophones moored on the seabed between Vestfjorden and the Barents shelf, and one model that listens to all of them at the same time.

      
The model does one thing: it separates the sound an animal makes from the sound a ship makes. When a blue whale is calling inside a shipping lane, the bridge of the nearest vessel is told before the watch changes. Everything the network hears is published — see open data.

    

  


  

    
◎How it listens

    
    
      
  • 01Hydrophone. 24-bit, 96 kHz, moored between 60 and 400 m. Eleven months on one battery pack, recovered by acoustic release each September.
    • 
      
  • 02Edge. Thirty-second windows become 128-bin mel spectrograms on the mooring itself. Nothing raw ever leaves the seabed.
    • 
      
  • 03Model. 4.1M parameters, nineteen classes, 380 ms per window on a 0.4 W core. It sends four hundred bytes where the recording would be forty megabytes.
    • 
      
  • 04Surface. Acoustic modem to a surface buoy, Iridium to the shore station. Median delivery from call to our rack: fifty-one seconds.
    • 
      
  • 05Bridge. Any vessel within twenty kilometres receives an AIS text message with bearing and species. Median call-to-bridge: ninety seconds.
    •

↯Ship alerts

    

      
Nineteen operators subscribe. The message that reaches the bridge has three fields: species, bearing, distance. There is no dashboard to open and nothing to install — it arrives on equipment already required by law.

      
Slowing to ten knots inside an alert box is voluntary. In 2026 it was chosen in three of four alerts, which is the number we care about most.

⌗Open data

    

      
Every accepted detection is public under CC-BY 4.0 within one hour, including the ones the model got wrong and we later corrected. Audio clips of the animal classes are released in the annual archive; vessel audio is not.

✎Working with us

    

      
We are nine: four on acoustics, three on the model, one on the cruises, one on the books. We hire twice a year and read everything. The mooring work is cold, wet and non-negotiable — everyone does one cruise.

      
Operators, ports and institutes can request the alert feed. Write to station@sonde.no and say which waters you work in.
