Updates

Sowing is closed for season 3. Nine hundred and forty people asked for a box and two hundred got one, which is a worse ratio than last year and the reason we are writing down every rejection.

The packets went out on 14 March, two weeks earlier than in 2025, after eleven gardeners in the north told us the soil was ready before the boxes were. That note is the whole point of the logbook.

Four varieties from season 2 are not in the boxes this year. Three of them failed on clay in more than half the plots, and one turned out to be the same cultivar under two names — which we only found because forty people photographed the same leaf.

How the trial works

  1. ①A box in March. Twelve packets, a soil card, and a logbook with a page per variety. Nothing digital is required until autumn.
  2. ②Four entries per variety. Date sown, date of first true leaf, how many of the ten came up, and one sentence in your own words.
  3. ③A photograph in July. One frame per plot, from standing height, with the logbook page in it. That is how a variety gets identified when the name on the packet is wrong.
  4. ④The dataset in October. Everything published, including the plots where nothing came up. A trial that only reports its successes is a catalogue.
