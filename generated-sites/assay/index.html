What you actually own

A bar, by serial

Allocated, not pooled. Your holding is a list of bars with serial numbers, not a share of somebody's inventory.

A shelf, not a screen

The metal sits outside the balance sheet of the custodian. If we close tomorrow, the bars are still yours and still there.

A weight, to 0.1 g

Re-weighed on intake and at every quarterly audit. The difference between the two numbers is published even when it is zero.

Four steps, one of them slow

01
You are checked, and it takes a week

Source of funds, identity, and the reason you want metal rather than a fund. Nobody is allocated on the same day, including the people who ask twice.

02
Bars are bought at the fix, not at a spread

We buy at the London afternoon fix and show you the invoice from the refiner. Our fee is 0.65 % a year and appears nowhere else.

03
The serials land in your ledger

Photographed on intake, weighed on the vault's scale, entered under your name. You get the shelf and the weight, not a certificate of participation.

04
You can take it home

Withdrawal is a booking, not a request. Bring an ID and something to carry a kilogram in; the customs paperwork is yours from the door onward.

The list for spring
