No admin key

Nobody can move the money alone

Five of nine signers, published names, published addresses. Two of the nine have never worked on the project and were chosen precisely for that. A signer who stops answering is replaced by a vote, and the vote is a transaction too.

Milestones

Written by the person doing the work

Applicants define what «done» means for each tranche. We can argue about it before signing, and we do; after signing, the definition is fixed and neither side can quietly move it.

Returns

Coming back with money is a good outcome

Two million dollars have been returned unspent by people whose work turned out cheaper or smaller than planned. Returning is recorded as a completed grant, not a failed one, and it counts in your favour next time.

Six rules, and the ones that hurt us are still there.

They were written in the first year, when the treasury was small enough that a bad decision would have ended it. They have not been edited since, and every proposed edit is in the repository along with why it was rejected.

Read the charter
  • 01
    Publish the refusalsEvery declined application is published with the reason, unless the applicant asks otherwise. Fourteen this quarter; three were declined because we did not understand the work, and it says so.
  • 02
    No grants to signersNobody who holds a key can receive money, and neither can a company they own. This has cost us two good projects and the rule stays.
  • 03
    Anonymity is allowedA recipient may stay anonymous to the public, though not to the signers. Seven grants are held this way; the work is judged the same and the transactions are just as visible.
  • 04
    The treasury holds no token of oursStablecoins and one blue-chip asset, rebalanced quarterly by a published rule. A foundation that funds itself with its own token funds itself with a rumour.
  • 05
    Grant sizes are capped at 5 %No single grant exceeds five per cent of the treasury at the time of signing, whoever asks and however good it sounds in the room.
  • 06
    Sunset by defaultIf the treasury falls below a year of committed obligations, the foundation stops accepting applications and finishes what it owes. Ending on time is part of the design.

If you maintain something that everybody imports and nobody pays for, write to us.

Applications are a page long. We answer within fourteen days, we say no more often than yes, and either way you will get the reason in writing rather than silence.
