The whole spec

FabricCotton drill 380 g/m², sanforised, woven in Ivanovo, undyed and then piece-dyed in three colours only.
SeamFlat-felled, double needle, 4.2 mm pitch. Shoulders and side seams closed twice; no overlock anywhere on the garment.
ThreadPolyester core-spun 40/2, tex 40. Rated above the fabric so a seam never tears the cloth when it goes.
HardwareBrass shank buttons, 18 mm, replaceable by hand with a pin and a hammer. Rivets at both pocket mouths.
ShrinkageUnder 2% in length after five washes at 40°. Measured on ten garments, not calculated.
RepairSend it back at any age. Pockets, cuffs and elbows are replaced at cost; the first repair in eight years is free.
