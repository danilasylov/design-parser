Reading is a thing you do together, out loud, on Wednesdays

Primer is a reading club for children of five to eight and whoever brings them. One book a fortnight, read at home in small pieces and finished together in a room with a carpet, a librarian and eleven other families.

Come to a trial Wednesday See this year's books

Three things happen every fortnight

A book arrives

Posted to the house, one copy per child, with four bookmarks that mark where to stop. Nobody has to read ahead and nobody has to catch up.

You read four evenings

Ten minutes each, out loud, taking turns. The grown-up reads the hard page; the child reads the page with the dog on it.

Wednesday, everyone talks

An hour in the library. We argue about the ending, draw the part nobody liked, and the librarian never asks what the author meant.

We do not teach reading. We give it somewhere to go.

Children who can read perfectly well often stop, because nothing happens after the last page. Primer is the thing that happens after: eleven other people who read the same book and disagree about it.

The year, fortnight by fortnight

September – November Six picture books with almost no words, so the youngest ones do the reading and the grown-ups have to listen. 6 meetings
December – February Four longer stories in chapters. This is where children discover a book can be waited for. 5 meetings
March – May Everyone writes and staples one book of their own, eight pages, and reads it to the room. Spelling is nobody's business. 6 meetings

Books we are reading now

Fortnight 03

The Boy Who Kept a Crow

Forty pages, one bird, and an argument about whether keeping a wild thing is ever kind. Half the room says yes.

Fortnight 04

Everything on Our Street

A picture book with no story, only a street drawn eleven times across a year. The children find what changed.

Twelve families. Two places left for spring.

Come to one Wednesday for free before you decide. If your child hides under the table the whole hour, that is data and we will tell you honestly whether to come back.
