Sixteen pages, one street, two inks, every Thursday.

We walk it end to end, knock on the doors that open, count what is actually there, and print the result on the machine in the back room. Nothing is written from a desk, and nothing is written twice.

Read a sample issue
01

Monday, on foot

One reporter, one street, both pavements. We count the shutters that never open, the trees, the benches, and the names still painted above the doors.

02

Wednesday, on the drum

Two passes of ink — blue first, then the second pass slightly out of register, which is what makes a risograph look like a risograph and not like a laser printer.

03

Thursday, in the post

Four hundred copies, folded by hand at the long table, in the post by five. What is left goes to eleven shops and nowhere else.
