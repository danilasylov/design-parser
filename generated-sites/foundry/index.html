Method

How the interviews are made, and what we refuse to do

The rules were written after the first eleven interviews, when we realised how much a question can put into a person's mouth. They are published because a reader is entitled to know how a sentence in an issue came to exist.

01

The chronology first, the feelings later

The first session only establishes dates, shops, and names — nothing is asked about meaning. People remember far more precisely once the calendar is on the table.

02

No leading nouns

We do not say the words «tragedy», «collapse», or «golden years». If a person uses one, we ask what they mean by it and keep their definition.

03

The transcript goes back

Every speaker reads their own transcript and may strike anything, without explaining why. Fourteen people have removed passages; three removed the whole interview.

04

Silence is printed

Pauses over four seconds appear in the text as a bracketed count. Where a person cried or refused to continue, the page says so instead of tidying it away.

05

Nothing is composited

A quotation belongs to one person, one recording, one date. We never merge two answers into a smoother sentence, even when it would read better.

Read one issue first

Number 03 goes out on its own for 900 ₽ with postage. Most people who subscribe do it after that one, which is why we suggest it rather than the first.

The recordings are open

Audio and transcripts are free to read on the site and free to quote with attribution. The paper issue is what pays for the next forty interviews.

If you worked there

We record in your kitchen, not in a studio, and it takes two visits. Write to us and someone will call within a week — there are eleven people on the waiting list, and we are working through it.
